Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions

How do I connect my custom domain?
Go to your dashboard, select your website, and click "Domains". Follow the instructions to add your custom domain and update your DNS settings.
How do I upgrade my plan?
Visit our pricing page, select your desired plan, and follow the payment instructions. Your upgrade will be activated within 24 hours.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes! You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Your plan will remain active until the end of your billing period.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers, mobile money, cash payments, and all major credit cards. Contact us via WhatsApp or Telegram for payment details.
