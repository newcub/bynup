Best practices

Tips to reduce risk and increase conversions

Set a reasonable maximum order amount to minimize risk on individual deliveries.

Provide clear instructions about delivery hours and payment method (exact change, card on delivery, etc.).

Confirm payments promptly after delivery so your accounting stays accurate.

Track deliveries for accountability, especially in high-value areas.
