Setup guide

Get PayPal running in 5 steps

1

Open the PayPal Developer Dashboard and create a sandbox business account.

2

In My Apps & Credentials, create a new app and copy the Client ID and Secret.

3

Paste them into the form above in Sandbox mode and click Test connection.

4

Place a test order using a sandbox personal account and a test credit card.

5

When everything works, disable the mode toggle and paste your live credentials.

Pro tips

Best practices for PayPal integration

Always test in sandbox mode before switching to live. PayPal's sandbox is comprehensive — use it.

Store your live client secret somewhere safe (like a password manager) so you never lose access.

Monitor your PayPal account for unusual activity, especially in the first few weeks of going live.

PayPal business accounts have lower fees than personal accounts for accepting payments.
