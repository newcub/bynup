How it works

Order flow for social media payments

1

Customer selects "Social Media Payment" at checkout.

2

They enter their contact and delivery details.

3

The system creates a professional order summary.

4

Customer gets links to your social accounts with the pre-filled order.

5

Customer sends the order via their preferred platform.

6

You receive the order directly in your DM / inbox.
