Understanding tax rates

How the settings on this page work

Priority — lower numbers are calculated first. Use priority when multiple rates apply to the same jurisdiction.

Compound tax — when enabled, this tax is calculated on the total including other taxes.

Jurisdiction — define where this rate applies: country, state, city, or postal code.

Postal code matching — use regex patterns for complex rules, e.g. ^[0-9]{5}$ for US ZIP codes.

Validity period — set start and end dates for temporary rates or scheduled tax changes.
